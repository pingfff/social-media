Deloads: Match the Medicine to the Illness

Cut the lever your program actually stresses — every 8–12 weeks, or when symptoms cluster

VOLUME-DRIVEN PROGRAM

Cut sets ~50%,
keep the loads

High weekly-set programs stress recovery through total work — halve the work, keep the intensity signal.

INTENSITY-DRIVEN PROGRAM

Cut loads to ~60%,
keep the sets

Heavy top-sets stress the nervous system and joints — nothing above 90%, ever, on any deload.

THE FOOLPROOF VARIANT

Frequency deload:
stretch the week

Change nothing about the workouts — just spread the week across 8–12 days. My default with clients.

SYMPTOMS CHECKLIST — ANY CLUSTER MEANS INTERVENTION TIME

  • Weights that used to feel light now feel heavy
  • Soreness lingers longer than usual  ·  no pump, even on isolation work
  • Losing motivation to train; feeling run down; getting sick often — not "push through"
It should feel easy. If your light week still feels hard, you timed it perfectly — that's fatigue leaving. (~10 days fully off is where detraining begins.)
