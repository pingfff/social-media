Real Life: The Cheat-Sheet

If the large majority of your eating is on-plan, the minority can flex

✈️

Travel

Anchor protein at every meal; make smart choices where you can, then stop stressing.

Hotel breakfasts, restaurants, gas stations — every menu has a chicken, fish, or steak option. Choose the meal by its protein.

📅

Busy week

Keep two zero-effort protein options stocked — Greek yogurt tubs + a powder you like.

When life stacks up, the diet that survives is the one that requires zero decisions. Default to double portions of protein.

🍽

Social meal

One restaurant dinner doesn't undo a week — and one perfect week doesn't earn a lost weekend.

Enjoy it, order by the protein, and get back on-plan at the next meal. Consistency over months is the only currency that spends.

Your diet has to survive contact with reality — or it won't last long enough to matter.
