Which Split? The Decision Tree

Work top to bottom — the first YES wins

Training age under ~1 year?
→
Full Body, 3×/week
a little stimulus does a lot; no deloads needed yet
Sessions past ~1.5h, or back-half lifts stalling from fatigue?
→
Move up: Full Body → Upper/Lower → PPL
distribute the volume before it decays
Fresh at the end of every session?
→
Move down the ladder
concentrate stimulus quality — regression can be progress
Only ~45 minutes per session?
→
Body-part split days
max 2 muscle groups per day
Schedule can't commit to fixed weekdays?
→
Rotating PPL (3 on, 1 off)
or hybrid PPL with fixed rest days
One muscle badly lagging?
→
Specialization phase
cut a strong area to ~3 maintenance sets, reallocate to the priority at 2–3×/week
Healthy intermediate, no complaints?
→
Upper/Lower, 4 days
the intermediate default
The engine (effort, volume, progression) matters; the split is just the container. Volume needs dictate the split — never pick the split first.
