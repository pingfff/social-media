Energy Balance: The Lever That Decides Everything

Not how "clean" you eat, not meal timing — total calories set the position; everything else is fine-tuning

🍽️

Calories In

the energy in the food you eat — every meal, every day, adds to this side

vs
🔥

Calories Out

organs, brain, digestion, daily activity, and training all burn from this side

DEFICIT

→ Fat loss

in < out: your body pulls the shortfall from stored body fat. That's the entire mechanism.

MAINTENANCE

→ Stay

in = out: weight roughly stable — your baseline number to find first.

SURPLUS

→ Gain

in > out: building new tissue is energy-expensive — a small surplus supports it.

Two exceptions where muscle grows in a deficit: higher body fat (stored energy funds it) and novice/returning-from-layoff status.
