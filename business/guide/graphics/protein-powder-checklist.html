Protein Powder: The Buyer's Checklist

Not a magic muscle-builder — convenient food for hitting your Chapter 10 target (1–3 scoops/day)

✓
Complete protein source
Whey (concentrate is fine; isolate if dairy sits heavy) — or a pea + rice blend if dairy isn't for you.
✓
Check protein % of the scoop
Protein-per-serving vs scoop weight — that ratio is the honest price comparison.
✗
No amino spiking
Glycine, taurine, or alanine listed alongside the protein = the label number is inflated cheaply. Skip it.
✓
Taste you'll actually drink daily
The best powder on paper loses to the one you'll use every day. Timing doesn't matter.
Same filters everywhere: transparent doses, few ingredients, claims that sound boring.
