Bodypart Shortcuts: The Biggest Lever

One highest-agreement lever per muscle — condensed from the full knowledge base

Back

Mix vertical + horizontal pulling.

Rows at least match pulldowns; watch the >25 weekly-sets recovery cliff; grip choice shifts width vs thickness.

Chest

Press from multiple incline angles.

Machines and Smith presses earn their place; 2×/week beats one marathon session; sternum angle is individual.

Legs

Full depth + direct hamstrings.

Curl-first ordering works; quads through full range; calves need dedicated stretch work — daily life already loads them.

Shoulders

Side delts are the visual priority.

12–16 sets/week over 3 days; rear delts come along with rowing; pressing covers front delts amply.

Arms

Train the stretch: overhead & incline work.

3–6 direct sets/week is a real start — back and pressing cover plenty; specialize to 9–14 only when all else is settled.

All of them

Load the lengthened position.

The deep stretch beats the squeeze — depth, mobility, or a different exercise; every muscle agrees on this.
