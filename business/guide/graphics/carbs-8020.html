Where Your Carbs Come From

A carb is a carb — the difference is the packaging: what comes with the carbs

80–90%
EVERYDAY CARBS — THE BASE

Minimally processed whole foods

  • Fruit & vegetables
  • Oats  ·  rice  ·  potatoes & sweet potatoes
  • Beans & legumes  ·  whole grains

These bring fiber, micronutrients, and fullness — and mostly handle your ~14g fiber per 1,000 calories.

10–20%
FLEX CARBS

The garnish, not the meal

Bread, treats, whatever you actually enjoy.

Within your calories, it costs you nothing in progress.

Carbs don't make you fat — a calorie surplus does. Set protein and fat first; let carbs fill the rest.
