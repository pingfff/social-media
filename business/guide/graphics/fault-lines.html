Training to
failure?
 2–3 RIR camp: failure adds fatigue faster than growth. 0 RIR camp: growth spikes at failure (one 2022 meta; another found no advantage). VERDICTThe exercise decides: compounds stay 1–3 RIR; machines & isolations — fail the last set.
How much
volume?
 Mainstream: 10–20 hard sets/muscle/week; 20–30 for small muscles. Weekly-stimulus: past 10–12 sets returns are minuscule; 6–8 quality sets can out-grow sloppy 20s. VERDICT10–20 is a ceiling you earn, not a target you owe — high ends assume elite/assisted recovery.
Bro splits —
dead or fine?
 Critics: 1×/week frequency plateaus advanced trainees; 2–3×/week gives higher-quality sets. Defenders: with effort + total volume in place, it works — especially for high per-session intensity. VERDICTFrequency is a distribution tool: 8+8 beats 16-in-one-session. The split you'll run beats the one you won't.
