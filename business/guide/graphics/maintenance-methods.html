Step 1: Find Your Maintenance Calories

Two practical ways to estimate the intake where your weight stays stable

METHOD 1 — MORE ACCURATE

Track a normal week

📱

Weight stable and diet fairly consistent? Log a normal week in an app and average it.

That average is your approximate maintenance — built from your real life, not a formula.

✓ BUILT FROM REAL DATA
METHOD 2 — FAST START

Use a calculator

💾

Know your body fat %? Katch-McArdle is the better pick.

Don't? Use Harris-Benedict. Either gets you close enough to start — real-world data corrects it in Step 3.
