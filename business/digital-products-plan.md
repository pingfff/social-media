# Fitness Business Expansion Plan — Digital Products First

*Drafted 2026-09-24. Premise: online coaching is the current revenue engine; the
goal is to add scalable, non-time-bound income streams that leverage existing
assets — the knowledge base (`knowledge/`), the split-creator skill, and the
content pipeline (series/, storyboard/) — before committing to heavy builds
(custom app) or physical inventory (supplements, apparel).*

## Guiding strategy

1. **Digital before physical.** Digital products are built once, sold
   repeatedly, no inventory. Physical (supplements/apparel) is parked until
   audience data justifies it.
2. **Sell the engine, not just outputs.** The split-creator pipeline turns a
   1:1 coaching deliverable into a 1:many product. Every product should be a
   packaging of that pipeline, not a one-off effort.
3. **Ladder, not leap.** Cheap product → flagship → membership → (maybe) app.
   Each step funds and de-risks the next.
4. **Contradictions as content moat.** Most competitors present one guru's
   view. We can present sourced positions and the context behind disagreements
   (natural vs assisted, novice vs advanced). This framing carries through
   products and social content alike.

## Phase 1 — Launch the storefront (target: 2–4 weeks)

**Goal: first non-coaching revenue with minimum build effort.**

- **P1.1 Flagship program (the "hero" product, $49–79).**
  Pick the strongest existing generated program (candidates: `intermediate` or
  `denseframe`) → expand into a sellable deliverable:
  - 12-week hypertrophy program: overview, why-it-works (cited to knowledge
    base), full session tables, progression rules, deload protocol, exercise
    substitution guide, logging sheets.
  - Deliver as designed PDF (can be produced via the pdf skill) + optional
    spreadsheet tracker (xlsx skill).
- **P1.2 Entry product ($15–25): "Program Design Toolkit."**
  Training log spreadsheet + set/rep/RIR reference cards + split-selection
  decision tree (already exists as `07-program-selection-decision-tree.md`).
- **P1.3 Free lead magnet:**
  One-page "which split fits you" cheat sheet (from knowledge/07) → email capture.
- **Storefront:** Gumroad or Stan Store to start (zero maintenance). Custom
  site later.
- **Funnel:** social content (existing pipeline) → lead magnet → entry product
  → flagship.

**Exit criteria to move on:** ≥ 25 flagship sales or ~$1.5k revenue, and
recurring buyer questions that a membership would answer.

## Phase 2 — Recurring revenue: membership (target: month 2–3)

**Goal: predictable monthly income; prove demand for app-like delivery.**

- Platform: Skool, Discord, or Substack (~$10–25/mo tier).
- Content cadence (all sourced from the existing pipeline):
  - Monthly new program or specialization block (armory-style templates are
    cheap to generate now).
  - Monthly "contradiction breakdown" post (one row of 08-source-map § Known
    Contradictions → honest both-sides breakdown).
  - Q&A / form-check threads; program-template files for members.
- Position as "the evidence-sourced training community," not generic coaching.

**Exit criteria:** ~100 members (~$1.5–2.5k MRR) AND members asking for
logging/tracking in-app → justifies Phase 3.

## Phase 3 — Delivery layer: app decision gate (month 4–6)

Do NOT build an app first. At this gate, choose:

| Option | When | Cost/risk |
|---|---|---|
| White-label coach app (Dojo Fit / TrueCoach / BuildFire class) | < 150 members, want app UX without dev work | ~$50–300/mo |
| Custom Expo (iOS-first) prototype | Members actively demand custom features (program engine in-app) | Months of part-time vibe-coding; you own maintenance |
| Stay on membership platform | Members satisfied, revenue growing | Free |

Default: white-label first, custom app only after proven pull. A prototype
scope (v1) is pre-agreed if we go custom: browse programs, log workouts, one
subscription tier via RevenueCat. Nothing else in v1.

## Phase 4 — Scale plays (only with audience data)

- **Coach-facing course** ($200–500): "How to write hypertrophy programs" —
  teaches the split-creator methodology to other coaches. Higher ticket, and
  the knowledge base is uniquely strong here.
- **Affiliate stacking** (start immediately, zero cost): equipment/supplement
  links in content descriptions.
- **Supplements / apparel:** parked. Revisit only if audience surveys show
  demand AND audience is large enough to absorb inventory risk. Print-on-
  demand merch is the only acceptable early version.
