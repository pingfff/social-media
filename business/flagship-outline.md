# Flagship Product Outline — "Second Gear" 12-Week Hypertrophy Program

*Working draft for the Phase 1 hero product. Source program:
[programs/intermediate-2026-09-22.md](../programs/intermediate-2026-09-22.md).
Target buyer: intermediate lifter, 1–3 years training, 4 days/week available,
wants visible muscle growth. Pricing recommendation at the bottom.*

## Positioning (one line)

**A 12-week upper/lower hypertrophy program that tells you *why* every choice
was made — built from 40+ coach and science sources, including where the
experts disagree.**

Differentiators to hammer in marketing copy:
- Every set, rep range, and rest period has a reason, traceable to named
  coaches/researchers — no "trust me bro."
- The honest-contradiction angle: e.g. rear delts and calves — we show both
  expert positions and why we chose what we chose (unique to this knowledge base).
- Logbook-driven autoregulation: the numbers decide, not soreness or vibes.
- Session-length engineered to ~75 min, including supersets.

## PDF outline

1. **Cover + promise** — what you'll get out of 12 weeks; who this is for
   (and who it's NOT for: beginners, >3-day-per-week- unavailable lifters).
2. **How to read this program** — RIR explained simply; what a "hard set"
   means; why warm-ups never count.
3. **The weekly split** — Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri upper/lower; why deadlifts sit on
   Friday [T14]; why upper days alternate chest-led/back-led [T17].
4. **The four sessions** — full tables with exercises, sets × reps, RIR,
   rest, coaching notes, and substitutions for every exercise.
5. **Why this volume** — the per-muscle set counts explained (growth bands,
   indirect work counting, why arms get "only" 6 direct sets).
   **Includes the contradictions section:** rear delts (#9), calves (#10),
   failure training (#11) — both positions shown, our choice stated. This is
   the marketing-differentiating chapter.
6. **Progression: the two blocks** — calibration week → add reps (Block 1) →
   add sets (Block 2); double progression rules with worked examples.
7. **Deloads** — weeks 5 and 12; halve sets, keep loads; symptom override list.
8. **Autoregulation** — the adjustment triggers, verbatim from the program;
   worked example of "reps flat 2 sessions → drop 1 set."
9. **Frequently missed sessions** — how to handle 3-day weeks without losing
   the week's stimulus.
10. **After week 12** — re-test protocol; paths forward (run Block 2 again,
    move to the 3-day advanced program — cross-sell product #2).
11. **Appendix** — volume audit table (simplified), exercise substitution
    matrix, glossary.

## What changes from the internal program doc

- Strip lifter-specific profile references and intake assumptions; generalize
  ("if your recovery is good…").
- Citations become friendly: "Jeff Nippard-style volume recommendations"
  instead of raw `[T4]` codes. Keep a sources page mapping claims → sources.
- Add buyer-facing elements the internal doc lacks: worked examples, FAQ,
  missed-session playbook, log sheets.
- **Re-audit after generalization** — volume/schedule assumptions in the
  internal doc were profile-specific.
