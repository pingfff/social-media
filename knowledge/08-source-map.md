## Known Contradictions Between Sources (kept, not resolved)

1. **Failure training:** T3 cites pro-failure meta-analysis (last set to failure) vs T1's consensus (2–3 RIR suffices) vs T21 (always to failure) vs T16/T2 (novices/effort moderation).
2. **Bro split:** T19/T20 defend it (effort + preference) vs T18/T21 (plateaus advanced trainees; ~every-5-days frequency better).
3. **Frequency:** T1/T6/T18 (doesn't matter if volume equated) vs T22 (misleading meta-analyses; low-volume high-frequency may be superior) vs T18 (advanced need 2–3×/wk).
4. **Volume norms:** T6 (20–30 sets/wk small muscles) vs T22 (>10–12 sets/wk = minuscule ROI). Elite/assisted vs natural contexts explain much of the gap.
5. **Eccentrics:** T3 (slow negatives, eccentrics "more important") vs T4 (10 s eccentric adds nothing; damage isn't a driver).
6. **Per-session set ceiling:** T22 (~6 sets) vs T23 (15–16 sets/session ceiling in meta-analysis).
7. **Deload necessity:** T15/T6 (regular scheduled deloads) vs T16 (novices don't need them).
8. **Arm/limb volume:** arms/1 (3–6 direct sets/wk suffices) vs arms/3 specialization (9–14 sets/wk) vs T6 (20–30 sets/wk small muscles). Context-dependent: baseline vs lagging-part focus.
9. **Rear delts:** shoulders/2 (overrated if back training is good) vs shoulders/3/4/6 (start every session with them).
10. **Calf training:** legs/1 (dedicated volume + stretch holds) vs legs/3 (gym work mostly tendon health; growth from life load).
11. **RIR:** back/2 (prescribed RIR makes people undertrain; set 2 to failure) and chest/1/3, shoulders/3 (0 RIR on machines) vs 2–3 RIR baseline — converging theme: machines/cables permit failure, heavy compounds don't.
12. **Lengthened-partials hype:** back/2 (no difference in trained lifters — tempered) vs shoulders/4, chest/2 (stretch position = most important ROM).
