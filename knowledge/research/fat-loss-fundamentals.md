# Fat Loss Fundamentals — Evidence Summary

## Deficit setup

- 10-25% below maintenance (~300-750 kcal). Default sweet spot: ~500 kcal.
- Rate of loss: 0.5-1.0% bodyweight/week. Lean/intermediate: 0.5%.
  Higher bodyfat tolerates 1%.
- Protein: 2.2-3.1 g/kg. Fat floor: ~0.5-0.6 g/kg (hormones).
  Carbs: whatever remains, kept higher around training.

## Why progress stalls (physiology, not failure)

- Adaptive thermogenesis: NEAT drops, TDEE falls 50-150+ kcal as you diet.
- Scale noise: water, glycogen, sodium, bowel content, stress/cortisol.
  Use WEEKLY AVERAGES, never daily readings. "Whoosh" effect is real
  water masking fat loss for days.

## Refeeds and diet breaks

- Refeed: 1-2 days at maintenance, carbs up/fat down. Main benefit =
  training quality + psychology; small metabolic effect.
- Diet break: 1-2 weeks at maintenance every 6-12 weeks of dieting.
  MATADOR study (Byrne 2018): intermittent deficit (2wk on/2wk off) =
  better retention and slightly more fat loss than continuous.

## Reverse dieting

- Post-cut: increase ~100-150 kcal/week back to maintenance/new surplus.
- Evidence for "metabolic repair" is limited — real value is preventing
  post-diet/post-show rebound binging. (Creator literally did this on
  camera after a week of eating whatever — authentic angle.)

## Cardio

- Steps first: NEAT is the biggest lever (7-10k+ daily).
- LISS vs HIIT: same fat loss when calories matched — pick the one you
  will actually do. Keep hard cardio away from leg training (interference).

## Preserving muscle on a cut

- Keep intensity (load on the bar); cut volume ~1/3 only if recovery
  demands it. Strength loss is expected and returns fast.

## Hunger management

- Volume eating (potatoes, veg, berries), protein + fiber per meal,
  diet soda/black coffee/zero-sugar energy drinks, sleep 7-9h.
  Sleep restriction reliably increases next-day intake ~300-400 kcal.
