# Supplements — Quick Reference (honesty tier)

## Tier 1 — strong evidence

- Creatine monohydrate: 3-5g/day, timing irrelevant, loading optional
  (20g/day x 5-7 days). Most researched supplement in existence.
- Caffeine: 3-6 mg/kg pre-training. Cut off ~8h before bed (half-life).
  (Monster daily = canon, but know the dose math.)
- Protein powder: food, not magic — convenience tool to hit 1.6-2.2 g/kg.

## Tier 2 — situational

- Beta-alanine: 60-240s efforts, needs 8-14 weeks, tingles harmless.
- Citrulline malate 6-8g: modest pump/performance.
- Electrolytes/sodium: hard sessions, heat, low-carb phases.

## Honesty tier (creator takes these — frame carefully)

- Berberine: real glucose effects in metabolic dysfunction; "insulin
  sensitivity" benefits for lean healthy lifters = weak evidence.
  Frame as personal digestion/preference ritual, NOT magic.
- Apple cider vinegar: modest post-meal glucose blunting at best;
  fat-loss claims = hype.

## Not worth money

- Fat burners, BCAAs (if protein adequate), test boosters, detoxes,
  "anabolic" window products.

## Sleep > everything

- 7-9h. Sleep restriction shifts weight loss away from fat and blunts
  muscle gain. Best free "supplement" that exists.
