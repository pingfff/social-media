# The Truth About Peak Week

173K views, 3.21% engagement, 15 min staged lecture by John Meadows, 2017.

## Key claims

- Thesis from 60+ shows / 30+ years / pro coaching: "peak week is largely
  hogwash" — most competitors look better the week BEFORE or the week
  AFTER the show than on show day, because they chased magic protocols.
- Rule 1: be contest-lean TWO WEEKS out. No manipulation removes fat.
- Water: fix the variable — exact same intake every day from Monday
  (e.g. exactly 5 L) so Friday's small tweaks (±1 L) are predictable.
  Chaotic intake = unpredictable look. Watery: 5→4 L; flat: bump up.
- Sodium: change nothing — hold prep intake constant Monday→show. Cutting
  sodium (+ water, + diuretics) = flat, soft, "no pop." A full muscle
  presses against the skin and shows more detail. If travel/stress
  flattens you: add a little sodium WITH water to refill.
- Potassium: no special supplementation. Slow-K + Lasix era = headaches,
  cramps, dehydration. You can't pump a dehydrated muscle.
- Carbs: once lean, ramp GRADUALLY from Mon/Tue upward. If you spill
  over: pull carbs back or cut water ~20% (5→4 L) — "9 times out of 10
  that fixes it."
- Anti-depletion: contest prep already depleted you for weeks. Hard
  depletions cost bigger athletes DAYS of recovery — "there's a look
  missing from your muscles that takes time to come back." Super-
  compensation logic from distance runners doesn't transfer.
- Night before: non-damaging upper-body pump workout, ~6 sets per body
  part. Achieving a pump = proof you're hydrated and delivering glucose
  (anecdote: at a 212 show he couldn't pump until he drank 2 extra
  liters — that's the diagnostic).
- Day of: same mini-pump, fewer sets. "A flat muscle is a soft muscle; a
  full muscle is a hard muscle" — the pump stretches skin, reveals
  detail.
- Diuretics: retired from prescribing them — you can't predict which
  compartment the water leaves; even "potassium-sparing" drugs leave you
  flat. 2004 story: his coach flushed his diuretics down the toilet; his
  first diuretic-free adult show = top 5 Mr. North America, great pump,
  felt great. "Don't buy the hype."
