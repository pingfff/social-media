# How to Peak a Natural Bodybuilder with Prep Coach and IFBB Pro Jared Feather

194K views, 2.91% engagement, 41 min interview (Dr. Mike + IFBB Pro Jared Feather), 2021.

## Key claims

- A peak = max intramuscular glycogen + minimal subcutaneous water + small
  waist (don't overfeed the day before/of the show).
- Ceiling on the whole process: an extremely conditioned natural can look
  ~5% better from Monday to Saturday. Practice the peak 3-4 weeks out
  (during the last deload); don't experiment on show week.
- Protein: hold steady through the week; cut ~25% on carb-load days
  (slightly below ~1 g/lb) — you're hypercaloric, can't lose tissue, and it
  spares GI load. Keep familiar prep protein sources; honor client
  "bloating" preferences even when placebo.
- Carb template for naturals: mild early-week depletion (minimal carbs but
  NOT keto — keto-to-carb transitions misfire), then a 2-day load at
  10-15 g/kg lean mass on day 1 (e.g. 15), adjusting down (13, then 10).
- Why the dose is capped: total-body glycogen ceiling ≈ 23 g/kg lean tissue
  (~2,300 g for a 100 kg male). 4-day 1,000 g loads are wasted unless
  trained off; supercompensation only nudges the ceiling and varies by
  individual.
- If the athlete is already shredded on ~100 g/day: don't drop to 20 g —
  at that leanness muscle is already primed to store carbs.
- Fats: raise to maintenance (or slightly above) during low-carb days —
  supports intramuscular triglyceride storage; final 6-7 days at
  maintenance to protect tissue while deloading.
- Water: mild elevation early week (size-dependent; sodium must rise with
  water); Thu/Fri return to NORMAL, not zero — you're still diuresing
  while incoming carbs pull fluid into muscle. Glycogen binds ~3-4 g water
  per gram (old figure 2.7, rat-liver studies). Cottonmouth during the
  load while still drinking ~3 L is normal.
- Potassium: don't chase it; extra comes via carb food sources.
- Training: deload starts 6-7 days out; 1-2 hard sets per body part early
  week; do NOT get sore — muscle damage inhibits glycogen uptake. Light
  pump work Wed/Thu (2-3 sets/body part, ~6 RIR) primes glycogen storage.
  Friday: posing only.
- Last-week fat loss: optional Mon/Tue only; usually not worth the
  cortisol. Cardio off by ~Wednesday. Maintenance + deload instead.
- Day-of: naturals can adjust on the fly; no 2-hour photo-driven
  corrections — the diuretic response must finish playing out.
- Mistake 1 — glucose disposal agents / "slin" supplements (berberine
  etc.): unstudied, potentially liver-risky, sometimes spiked with
  metformin (natty status gone); they don't "push" or "shunt" carbs.
- Mistake 2 — pumping up too early: burns glycogen. Pump only when your
  class lines up: push-ups + fast-GI carb (Gatorade) 10-15 min before
  stage = fuller, more vascular, holds poses. The 35-minute pumper fades
  on stage while everyone else sharpens.
- Mistake 3 — naturals cutting water AND sodium from Tuesday, zero on
  show day: naturals don't retain like enhanced athletes; aldosterone
  rebound + cortisol = worse look. Stop pulling water out of naturals.
- Post-show: skip the binge frame; go straight to maintenance with clean
  whole foods (~300-350 g carbs/day for a ~68 kg man), water and veggies
  first. Against both face-stuffing rebound AND traditional slow reverse
  dieting.
- Sources cited: Scott Stevenson peaking paper (Schoenfeld/Aragon
  foreword; free online), Eric Helms peaking work, Stevenson on Revive
  Stronger.
