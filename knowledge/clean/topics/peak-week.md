# Peak Week — what works on YouTube

Synthesis from the 2026-09-12 research run. Feeds the `peak-week` series.

## Winning formats

1. Full-day-of-eating carb-load content — CBum "FULL DAY OF EATING | CARB
   LOAD BEFORE SHOW DAY" 2.87M views / 3.07%; Lee Lem 1-min FDOE 424K /
   6.57%; Alay Shah short 555K / 3.3%. Exact-numbers format + carb
   content = the topic's view ceiling.
2. Shorts on single manipulations — Casey Kelly "Carb Up Grocery Haul"
   468K / 4.94% and "Depleted Carb Pumps" 395K / 5.86%; Alay Shah salt/
   water explainer 1.99M / 3.23% (Hindi — signals huge global appetite);
   Jermaine Gadsden 30s natural tips 22-24K each. Short-form engagement
   on this topic runs 4.9-6.6% — well above long-form.
3. Personal protocol explainers with the numbers in the title — Greg
   Doucette "My PEAK WEEK Explained!!! What I eat, cutting water, sodium,
   carb deplete, carb load, dry out" 533K / 3.11%.
4. Authority talk/interview — RP/Jared Feather natural peaking 194K /
   2.91%; Meadows "The Truth About Peak Week" 173K / 3.21%; Layne "Peak
   Week Mistakes" 98K / 2.68%. NOTE: these are 2017-2021 videos still
   owning the evergreen top spots — low content decay, open lane for a
   current natural/Classic Physique take.
5. Question-title myth-busters — Nippard "Should I Cut Sodium and Water?"
   96K (2014, still pulling views).

## Hook patterns

- Cold-open payoff clip before the intro (RP file) — show the anecdote
  result, earn it later.
- Anti-protocol thesis drop (Meadows: "peak week is largely hogwash") —
  iconoclasm from stacked credentials.
- Credential + pattern-interrupt in one breath (Layne: "500+ clients...
  I don't cut water or sodium — here's why").
- Broadened stakes (Meadows: contest OR photoshoot OR wedding) pulls in
  non-competitors.

## Protocol consensus & contradictions (the episode fuel)

Consensus across all three distilled sources:
- The physique is built BEFORE peak week. Arrive stage-lean (Meadows: 2
  weeks out). Peak week is worth ~5% at best (RP) and can easily subtract
  more than it adds (all three).
- NEVER hard-cut water or sodium for a natural. Layne: 70/30 intra/
  extracellular split means water cuts drain muscle more than sub-Q;
  aldosterone doubles by day 2 of a sodium cut and peaks on show day.
  Meadows: hold sodium constant; RP: only mild co-manipulation (water up
  = sodium up), return to normal, never zero.
- Don't be sore/damaged in the final week — muscle damage inhibits
  glycogen uptake (RP); pump workouts only, ~6 sets/body part, non-
  damaging (Meadows); deload 6-7 days out (RP).
- Backstage pump = short and late (RP: only when your class lines up,
  10-15 min, fast carbs; Meadows: mini-pump day-of). The 35-minute
  pumper fades on stage.
- No diuretics, no "slin"/GDA supplements for naturals (RP, Meadows).

Contradictions (dueling-takes episodes):
- Water load-then-normal (RP) vs. flat consistent intake all week
  (Meadows, Layne). All reject cutting; they differ on loading.
- Carb depletion: RP keeps a mild early-week depletion + 10-15 g/kg
  2-day load; Meadows rejects deliberate depletion ("you've already been
  depleting for weeks") and ramps gradually. Big-muscle athletes recover
  from depletion slowest (Meadows).
- Fiber: only Layne flags it — low-residue carbs, minimal fiber final
  24 h, no broccoli backstage.
- Post-show: RP/Layne/Nippard (lean-forever file) all reject slow
  traditional reverse dieting → go straight to maintenance with clean
  food. Consistent with our cutting-101 take.

Key numbers bank (kg-first): glycogen ceiling ~23 g/kg lean tissue;
carb load 10-15 g/kg day 1, then down; protein -25% on load days;
glycogen binds 3-4 g water/g; muscle ~70% water intracellular; aldosterone
2x by day 2 of sodium cut; maintenance calories final 6-7 days; stage-lean
2 weeks out; pump test = hydration diagnostic (2 L fixed a missing pump).

## Saturated / skip

- Generic show-day FDOE vlogs (CBum owns the format; only viable with a
  natural-numbers twist — g/kg, kg bodyweight).
- "Everything about peak week" listicles — old winners + huge Hindi
  channels saturate; a distilled natural/CP version can still win on
  angle, not breadth.
- Teaching aggressive water cuts/diuretics as how-to — dangerous, all
  three authorities call it the mistake; myth-bust framing only.
- Enhanced-athlete protocols repackaged for naturals — off-brand for IPB
  natural audience.
