# Protein — Evidence Summary

## Key numbers (daily total)

- Baseline recommendation: 1.6-2.2 g/kg bodyweight/day (Morton 2018
  meta-analysis breakpoint ~1.62 g/kg; upper safe/practical ~2.2).
- NEWER DEBATE (SBS, Dec 2024): the 1.6-2.2 rule is less certain than
  claimed — slightly higher intakes may be needed to truly max out gains.
  Position as "2.2+ doesn't hurt while cutting".
- Cutting (calorie deficit): 2.2-3.1 g/kg (Helms) — leaner = higher end.
- Bulking: ~1.6-2.2 g/kg is sufficient; more = mostly waste/satiety cost.

## Per meal

- 0.4-0.55 g/kg per meal, 3-5 meals.
- Practical floor: ~25-40g quality protein per meal (~2.5-3g leucine).
- Even distribution across the day beats front/back-loading.

## Timing

- Total daily intake >> nutrient windows. Pre/post workout shake timing
  is flexible (hours don't matter much if total is hit).
- "Anabolic window" as commonly sold = mostly myth.

## Other

- Plant protein: slightly larger doses needed (lower digestibility).
- Whey iso vs concentrate: allergy/lactose choice, not results choice.

## Script angles

- "You need LESS protein than gurus say, but MORE than the RDA"
- "Your 60g shake is mostly expensive pee"
- "Protein while cutting: the one number that saves muscle (2.2-3.1 g/kg)"
