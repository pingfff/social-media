# YouTube Transcript Wishlist

How to get: open video -> "..." -> Show transcript -> copy -> paste into
new folder here as transcript.txt (mirror the brand/ folder structure).
Header with source URL + view count + why it's in the library.

## For Cutting 101 (priority)

- Jeff Nippard — search his channel: "lose fat", "cutting", "calorie
  deficit" (his science-based cutting videos are the benchmark)
- Sean Nalewanyj — "cutting without losing muscle", simple meal plans
- Mario Tomic — intermediate fat loss, cardio and step strategies

## For Hypertrophy 101 / Program Design 101

- Jeff Nippard — volume, progressive overload, full body vs upper lower
- Renaissance Periodization (Mike Israetel) — volume landmarks, deloads,
  "training mistakes" series; ALSO grab his free Hypertrophy Training
  Guide article series if accessible
- Natural Hypertrophy — minimalist training, natural volume tolerance
  (best fit for the brand's natural positioning)
- Geoffrey Verity Schofield — programming for naturals

## For skits / humor study

- BroScience Life (Dom Mazzetti) — the classic gym-bro satire format
- Noel Deyzel — skit/short-form hybrid humor

Grab 3-5 per series BEFORE outlining it. One high-performing video per
creator is enough to extract hook + structure patterns.
