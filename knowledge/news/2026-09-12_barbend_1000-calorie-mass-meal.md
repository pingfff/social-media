## Key points

- Clean bulk philosophy: "If you're 15% body fat or under and want to
  gain mass, don't be so cautious." Don't overthink or eat too clean.
- Chicken breast/thighs + pasta = easiest calorie-dense combo. Carbs are
  protein-sparing (less protein burned for fuel = more for growth).
- Prep: pre-cut chicken, marinate (olive oil, spices, salt), medium
  heat; pasta + olive oil + tomato sauce simmered thick; cheese on top
  for extra calories; add veggies for micros.
- Eat every 2-3 hours during a bulk — hunger should return that fast.
- Best quote: "Make your food taste good, or you won't last. Don't try
  to tough it out. It doesn't make you more hardcore."
