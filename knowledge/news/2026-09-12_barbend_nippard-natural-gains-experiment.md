# Jeff Nippard's 14-Month Natural Gains Experiment

source: https://barbend.com/news/how-much-muscle-did-jeff-nippard-gain-in-14-months-naturally/
fetched: 2026-09-12
why: fuels Hypertrophy 101 + soft-season arc; realistic natural gain rates
for an intermediate/advanced audience

## The experiment

- Jan 2024 - Mar 2025. Jeff Nippard (natural, 15 yrs training) + his
  untrained brother Brad, same program, DEXA + ultrasound testing.

## Protocol

- Training: 2 upper-lower strength + 3 PPL hypertrophy sessions/week,
  2 rest days. Last set of EVERY exercise to failure. Proven exercises
  only. Strict progressive overload.
- Bulk: 3,000 kcal (150P / 410C / 85F) = 15% above maintenance, target
  ~2 lbs/month gain.
- Recomp: 2,800 kcal (160P / 335C / 85F) for 3 months.
- Sleep tracked (Oura). Supplements: creatine, whey, caffeine, fish oil,
  magnesium, vit D, multivitamin, ashwagandha.

## Results

- 1 month: Brad +12.7 lbs lean (mostly water/creatine), Jeff +3 lbs.
- 6-month bulk: Jeff +4.5 lbs lean, +11 lbs FAT (15% -> 20%+ BF — "a bit
  much"). Bulk was too aggressive.
- Recomp at maintenance: essentially zero new muscle. "At my level of
  advancement, you need a caloric surplus."
- FINAL (14 months, back at same bodyweight): Jeff +2.7 lbs lean
  retained, BF 15.8% -> 14.5%. Brad: +10 lean / -10 fat.

## Key takeaways for scripts

- Advanced natural gaining reality: ~1-1.5 kg lean PER YEAR with
  everything done perfectly. Reality-check content.
- Recomp works for beginners, NOT for advanced lifters — surplus needed.
- Aggressive bulking = mostly fat (2.5:1 fat:muscle ratio on 15% surplus).
- "You can push your natural ceiling up with optimized training and diet."
