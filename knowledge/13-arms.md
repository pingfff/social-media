# Arm Training: Biceps, Triceps, Brachialis

*Source: Program Design/arms/1–3 (anti-gimmick arm guide, J3U 4-exercise arsenal, 18-inch arm specialization)*

## Anatomy & Head-Targeting Reality

- **Biceps:** both heads are elbow flexors — **head isolation is clickbait**; both heads fully taxed near failure on any curl [arms/1]. Grip determines auxiliary muscles: supinated = max biceps; neutral = brachialis; pronated = brachioradialis [arms/1, arms/2]. Wrist must stay LOCKED (wrist flexion lets you move more load with less biceps tension) [arms/2].
- **Triceps = ~2/3 of upper-arm mass**, roughly equal in volume to pecs/lats [arms/1]:
  - Medial/lateral heads: elbow extension only — covered by ALL pressing
  - **Long head crosses the shoulder** → in presses, shoulder flexion lengthens it while elbow extension shortens it → length barely changes, tension stays LOW → **pressing alone neglects the long head; overhead/stretch isolation is essential** [arms/1, arms/2]

## Biceps Exercise Selection

Three criteria [arms/1]: (1) supinated grip, (2) joint comfort for long-term progression, (3) **load the bottom half of the ROM** (biceps grow best at long lengths).

**Ranked picks:**
1. **Bayesian cable curl** — fully lengthened + loaded; setup: upper arm meets cable at 90° at start; tension decreases as it shortens = resistance profile matches strength curve. Check alignment: cable runs elbow → middle of hand → delt [arms/2]
2. **Preacher curl** — heaviest at bottom, pad = strictness; covers mid-range + full SHORTENED position. Pairing Bayesian + preacher covers the full contractile range [arms/1, arms/2]
3. **Basic barbell/DB curls** — mid-range focus; perfectly viable for years [arms/1]; incline DB curls (30°) = lengthened staple [arms/3]
4. Crossbody hammer curl = brachialis (arm width/detail) [arms/2]

## Triceps Exercise Selection

1. **Pushdowns** (arm at side) = #1 go-to: best long-head leverage for elbow extension with shoulder neutral; long head still works here. Alignment: cable through delt → elbow → wrist; cue "drive hand back to the hip," not toward the floor [arms/1, arms/2]
2. **Overhead extensions** = fully lengthen the long head — essential to cover what pressing misses; cable cuff/daisy chain far more elbow-friendly than skull crushers; elbow fixed high and locked [arms/2]
3. Skull crushers = third option; to-forehead (pure extension) or behind-head (elbow-friendlier) [arms/1, arms/3]
- Close-grip bench/dips/JM press: fine but medial/lateral-biased — never sufficient alone [arms/1, arms/2]
- Elbow-pain fix: keep tension through the **ulnar (pinky) side** of the wrist, not thumb side (golfer's elbow prevention) [arms/2]

## Programming Numbers

| Variable | Prescription | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Biceps volume | **3–6 direct sets/wk** (up to 10; back work counts as indirect) | arms/1 |
| Triceps volume | **3–6 direct sets/wk** (pressing covers 2 heads) | arms/1 |
| Reps | **4–10** (higher end for novices/elbow issues) — isolations do NOT need high reps | arms/1 |
| Effort | ~1 RIR, sometimes failure — "if the bar doesn't slow down, you're going too easy" | arms/1, arms/3 |
| Frequency | ~2×/week stimulus | arms/3 |
| Limb-muscle rule | Biceps/triceps/calves/forearms grow better ISOLATED than compounds-only | arms/3 |

**Arm specialization protocol (arms/3):** upper/lower skeleton + 2 dedicated arm days; arms trained FIRST on upper days (fresh, tracked like main lifts — "treat curls like a competition lift"); start cycle with 2 sets/exercise, add sets over 8–12 weeks, deload at ~9–14 sets/wk/muscle; reverse exercise order between the two arm days; PPL compromise: biceps end of push day, triceps end of pull day. Progression: beat-the-book double progression; micro-plates help.

**Weak-point anti-bloat alternative:** cut 3 biceps exercises/workout → 1, train to genuine effort [arms/3].

## Technique & Mistakes

- Only the elbow joint moves (no swinging — "not a lower-back exercise") [arms/2]
- Elbows mostly stationary; dragging elbows forward = shoulder flexion = front delt steals work [arms/1]
- Triceps: shoulder locked, only elbow extends; don't turn extensions into mini-presses [arms/2]
- Slow negatives aren't extra growth — they're joint protection/form standardization [arms/1]
- Persistent tendonitis = change exercises, don't push through; rotate arm exercises every few months for injury prevention [arms/3]
- 1.25-lb micro plates enable slow dumbbell progression [arms/3]
- Strength standards (3×10, strict, intermediate): EZ curls 90 lb; incline DB curls 30 lb/hand; skull crushers 80 lb; overhead EZ extension 70 lb; pushdowns ~70 lb single-pulley [arms/3]

## Contradictions Logged

- arms/1 (3–6 sets/wk, low volume) vs arms/3 (up to 9–14 sets/wk in specialization) — baseline vs specialization contexts
- arms/1 reps 4–10 vs T21/T5-style 10–15 isolation norms — both within 6–30 equivalence debate
- Arms-first scheduling [arms/3] contradicts compounds-first convention — deliberate specialization inversion
- Cables preferred (arms/2, elbow health) vs free-weight staples (arms/1 "cables no real advantage") — comfort decides
