# Sleeve Season

**Lifter:** app template (no profile — generic intermediate assumptions in §9) · **Level:** Intermediate (2–4 yrs, post-foundation) · **Goal:** hypertrophy, **arms specialization block** · **Split:** U/L skeleton + 2 dedicated arm days (arms first on the upper day), 5 days/wk · **Session target:** 60 min · **Length:** 6 weeks = 4 overload + 1 deload + 1 re-test/exit

**What this program is:** A six-week arms specialization built on the arms/3 protocol: two dedicated arm days, plus arms trained *first* — treated like a competition lift — on the one upper day. The specialization is paid for honestly: chest and back drop to maintenance-level volume, and every freed set lands on biceps and triceps, which climb to 12 direct sets each by week 4. Sets start at 2 per exercise and get added weekly; when week 6's re-test shows arm measurements and arm-lift reps moving, you either run it again or return to a base program. If arms haven't moved by then, the volume trial failed and arms go back to 3–6 sets — the tape measure decides.

Chest/back deliberately compressed to one session — this is the maintenance trade the specialization makes [arms/3, 07-tree specialization branch].

## 4. Block structure & progression (6 weeks)

Intermediate block with the arms/3 set-add protocol [arms/3]:

- **Week 1 (calibration):** all arm exercises at **2 sets**, RIR 3 — treat every curl and extension as a logged competition lift [arms/3]. Everything else at table volume, RIR 3.
- **Weeks 2–4 (Method B, set-adds — arm-weighted):** add **+1 set/week to arm exercises** (lead pair first, cap 5/exercise [T6]) → week 4 = the tables shown (12 direct sets/muscle/wk). Keep loads where reps stay clean; micro-plates help [arms/3]. Beat-the-book double progression throughout [arms/3].
- **Week 5: deload.**
- **Week 6 (re-test + decision):** re-run week-1 loads on preacher curl, overhead extension, pushdown; measure both arms (same time of day). Decision rule in §6.

## 5. Deload plan

Volume deload, week 5: **halve sets, keep loads** [T15] — halve the arm volume too; the block's growth is banked here. Elbow warning signs (niggles on extensions) → localized deload instead: cut all triceps extension angles for the week, keep curls [T15 localized-deload menu]. Symptom override pulls it forward [T15, T20].

## 6. Autoregulation & adjustment triggers

- **Specialization kill-switch (week 6 re-test):** arm measurements or arm-lift reps unmoved → the volume trial failed [T18] — exit to a base program with arms at 3–6 direct sets [arms/1]; don't re-run the block louder. Moved → either run it once more (sets restart from 2/exercise) or exit while ahead [T2: volume is a phase dial].
- **Exit plan (mandatory regardless of outcome):** chest/back return to 10–20 sets across ≥2 sessions the week after the block ends — six weeks is the pay-cut ceiling, not a lifestyle.
- Elbow pain → rotate the exercise (every few months regardless) and keep tension on the ulnar side of the wrist [arms/2]; persistent tendonitis = change exercises, don't push through [arms/3].
- An arm exercise's reps flat/down 2 straight sessions → drop 1 set from the exercise before it, rerun [T18].
- Progressing on all lifts? Change nothing [T16]. Log every set [T6] — the logbook and the tape measure, not the mirror, judge this block.

## 7. Design rationale & contradictions

- **Decision-tree branch:** "One muscle/group badly lagging?" → specialization: strong areas cut toward maintenance, lagging part 2–3×/week [T17, T18 via 07-tree]. Container reshaped per the arms/3 protocol: U/L skeleton + 2 dedicated arm days + arms first on the upper day, order reversed between arm days [arms/3]. Level check: specialization is legitimate post-foundation [T21] — this template assumes the 2–4-yr intermediate, not a novice ("weak arms at 3 months means you have weak everything" [T21]).
- **Volume:** reallocation math — chest+back drop ~11 combined sets vs a standard intermediate week; arms gain ~13 combined direct sets; total systemic volume roughly flat (the guardrail of the 07 specialization branch).
- **Frequency:** arms 3×/week (Mon/Thu/Sat) — the 2–3×/wk small-muscle prescription [T18, arms/3].
- **Contradictions touched** (knowledge/08 § Known Contradictions):
  - **#4 volume norms:** arms/1's 3–6 baseline vs arms/3's 9–14 specialization vs T22's >10–12 ROI caution — position: the [arms/3] specialization protocol, time-boxed 6 weeks with an explicit kill-switch; week-4 peak of 12 stays under the 14 ceiling.
  - **#9 rear delts:** [shoulders/2] coverage position taken for this block only (0 direct sets over 6 back sets); documented, not resolved.
  - **#11 failure training:** arm isolations at 0–1 RIR per [arms/1] ("bar must slow down"); compounds 1–3 RIR [T3, T6 vs T21].
  - **Arms-first scheduling [arms/3] vs compounds-first [T4, T6]:** the documented inversion — deliberate specialization exception, not a general rule.
  - Rest standardized 2 min (vs 2–3 baseline [knowledge/02]) — the 60-min budget requires it; supersetting bi/tri is itself endorsed [T4, T23].

## 8. Assumptions

App template — no intake interview run. Defaults applied:

- Generic intermediate lifter (2–4 yrs structured training, past the foundation, one identified weak point: arms) → intermediate row of the level matrix; specialization permission granted by that level [T21]
- Full commercial gym, healthy elbows (elbow history → reroute: this block is extension-heavy [arms/2]), maintenance calories, good recovery, fixed 5-day schedule
- 60-min session cap
- No current-program Step 0 audit (template) — a real user's current chest/back volume must be checked before cutting it to 5–6: if they currently run 16+, taper down over the first week rather than dropping cold (the anti-atrophy check [T17])
- 6-week length per the request, validated against the level matrix (intermediate 4+1 fits with a 1-week re-test appended; the arms/3 protocol's full 8–12-week set-ramp is compressed — hence the restart option if week 6 says continue)
