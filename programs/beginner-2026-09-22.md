# Foundation 16

**Lifter:** [beginner](../profiles/beginner.md) · **Level:** Beginner (training age <1 yr) · **Goal:** hypertrophy · **Split:** Full body A/B/C, 3 days/wk · **Session target:** 60 min · **Length:** 16 weeks, no scheduled deloads

**What this program is:** Three full-body workouts a week (Mon/Wed/Fri), each built around the same handful of basic barbell, dumbbell, and machine lifts. Every week you simply try to get one more rep than last week with the same weight — that's the whole game. There are no deload weeks and no complicated changes, because in your first year the basics alone drive progress. If a lift feels heavier than it should for two sessions in a row, you drop one set from it and try again — the logbook decides, not how you feel. At week 16 you'll re-test your week-2 weights; if the reps have climbed, you've outgrown this program and move up to a 4-day split.

## 4. Block structure & progression (16 weeks)

Level matrix: beginner → linear/double progression, fast jumps, no deloads [T13, T15, T16].

- **Weeks 1–2 (calibration):** find loads that land each set mid-range at **RIR 3–4** — leaves progression room [T13-method A note]. Log every set's reps [T6].
- **Weeks 3–14 (overload):** **double progression** [T1, T2] — when you hit the **top of the rep range on all sets** of an exercise, add a set's worth of load next session (you choose the jump) and restart at the bottom of the range. Novices can also add 1–3 reps/set/week [T6-Method A]; never chase a calendar target — if reps don't move for 2 sessions, see §6.
- **Week 15:** hold loads; add 1 set to any lift that has hit the top of its range for 2 straight weeks (Method B lever [T6]).
- **Week 16 (re-test):** repeat week-2 loads and compare logged reps — this is the block's report card [T2: the logbook is the vehicle]. At week 16 the lifter graduates: re-run intake (likely intermediate → Upper/Lower container [07-tree, T22]).

## 5. Deload plan

**None scheduled** — novices accumulate too little stress to merit one [T15, T16]. **Symptom-triggered safety valve:** if the knowledge/06 warning signs appear (beat up, soreness lingering, light weights feel heavy, can't get a pump, performance declining [T15, T20]), take an unplanned deload that week: halve sets, keep loads (volume is the stressor in this program [T15]). The program still fits 16 weeks — the deload week replaces the current week and shifts the calendar.

## 6. Autoregulation & adjustment triggers

- Performance down or reps flat for 2 straight sessions → the prior session's volume/load was too high: drop one set from that exercise and rerun [T18, T8].
- Performance up → volume is right; keep everything unchanged — "if you're progressing, it's the right amount of work" [T16].
- Persistent joint pain on any movement → swap to the listed alternative (first filter = injury/pain [T6]); never train through joint pain.
- Logbook rule: log every set's reps [T6]; the logbook — not soreness or pump — is the judge [T16, T18, T19].

## 7. Design rationale & contradictions

- **Decision-tree branch:** training age <1 yr → Full Body 3×/wk [07-tree]; 3 days → full body (novice) per the container mapping; Q21 preference (full body) consistent.
- **16-week length validation** (level matrix): beginner + 16 wk = valid — novice linear progression lasts 3–6 months [T13, T16]; no deload weeks included, which the matrix requires for this level.
- **Volume:** conservative start below the 10–20 default band, per the matrix's beginner column and "a little stimulus does a lot" [T7-tree]; logbook calibrates upward.
- **Contradictions touched** (knowledge/08 § Known Contradictions):
  - **#4 volume norms (10–20 vs >10–12 diminishing ROI):** took the conservative side — beginner start below band; ROI argument supports it [T22 vs T1/T6].
  - **#10 calves:** both positions preserved — minimal dedicated sets with stretch pauses, flagged rather than resolved [legs/1 vs legs/3].
  - **#11 failure training:** took loose-RIR novice position (RIR 2–3 compounds, 1–2 isolations) — novices can't accurately gauge failure anyway [T3, T6 vs T21].
  - Rest standardized at 2 min per skill standard (vs 2–3 min baseline / 4–5 heavy legs [T4, knowledge/02]) — consistency and session budget won at this level.
