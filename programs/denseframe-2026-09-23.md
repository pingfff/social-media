# Dense Frame

**Lifter:** [denseframe](../profiles/denseframe.md) · **Level:** Advanced (10+ yrs) · **Goal:** hypertrophy (caloric surplus) · **Split:** Upper / Lower / Full-body, 3 days/wk · **Session target:** 75 min · **Length:** 12 weeks = 3 blocks of (3 overload + 1 deload)

**What this program is:** Three big sessions a week — an upper day, a lower day, and a full-body day — so every muscle gets exactly two hits weekly without needing more gym days. This is the advanced low-frequency compromise: fewer sessions than your body wants, so each one is full (~19–23 sets), built around a heavy barbell lift, and packed with supersets at the tail to fit the 75 minutes. Progress runs in three-week waves with a deload after each: first wave adds reps, second adds sets, third pushes closer to failure. With only three sessions, missing one costs half a muscle's week — the two non-negotiables are showing up and logging every set.

## 4. Block structure & progression (12 weeks)

Level matrix: advanced → 3 overload + 1 deload per block [T6]; levers rotate between blocks.

- **Block 1 — weeks 1–4 (Method A, reps):** week 1 calibrates at **RIR 3**; weeks 2–3 add **+1 rep per set** weekly (small advanced jumps [T6]); double progression when a range tops out [T1, T2].
- **Block 2 — weeks 5–8 (Method B, sets):** add 1 set/week to one exercise per session — **quads first** (they sit at the band floor), then whatever is still progressing (cap 5/exercise [T6]); reps restart from Block-1 week-2 levels.
- **Block 3 — weeks 9–12 (Method C, effort ramp):** sets frozen at Block-2 levels; RPE ramps **7 → 8 → 9** across weeks 9–11 [T6]. Week 12: deload + **re-test** — repeat Block-1 week-2 loads on incline press, pull-up, hack squat, RDL; logged reps there pick the next program [T2].

## 5. Deload plan

Volume deload, matched to the stressor: **halve sets, keep loads** [T15]. Weeks 4, 8, 12. With three long sessions, halving sets also shortens sessions — enjoy it; "if your light deload week still feels hard, you timed it right" [T15]. Symptom override (warning signs [T15, T20]) pulls a deload forward regardless of week.

## 6. Autoregulation & adjustment triggers

- An exercise's reps flat/down 2 straight sessions → prior volume too high: drop 1 set, rerun [T18, T8-knowledge]. At 2×/wk frequency this shows up fast — good.
- Progressing → change nothing [T16].
- Missing a session (life happens [T10]): run Friday's full-body day as the make-up — it touches everything; never stack two sessions in one day.
- PR-less for 8 weeks on a main lift → exercise swap signal [T6, T9–T11 context]. Joint pain → listed alternative immediately [T6].
- Log every set [T6]; the logbook judges [T16, T18, T19].

## 7. Design rationale & contradictions

- **Decision-tree branch:** advanced, healthy, 3 fixed days → 3-day container for intermediate+ is rotating U/L [07 mapping]; **reshaped to Upper/Lower/Full** — the rotation's frequency logic (each muscle 2×/wk) kept, but with an identical weekly layout so the volume audit is stable week to week and the schedule never rolls. Surplus + good recovery at 20s supports full 2× frequency on every muscle.
- **12-week validation** (level matrix): 3 × (3+1) advanced rhythm [T6].
- **Volume:** majors 10–12/wk (chest 10, back 12, hams 11, quads 9→Block-2 target); per-session peaks ≤7; arms direct 5 each + heavy indirect — the honest ceiling of a 3-day week, stated rather than padded.
- **Contradictions touched** (knowledge/08 § Known Contradictions):
  - **#1 frequency (barely matters volume-equated vs ≥2×/wk consensus):** resolved toward 2×/wk [T1, T5, T18] — the split's whole architecture depends on it; T22's high-frequency-low-volume position noted as an alternative 3-day build (full body 3×), rejected here because per-muscle exercise variety would collapse to one exercise per angle per week.
  - **#4 volume norms:** majors held 10–12, mid-band [T1/T6 vs T22].
  - **#9 rear delts:** "covered by back training" side [shoulders/2] — 12 back sets.
  - **#10 calves / abs at retention:** both positions preserved; maintenance chosen [legs/1 vs legs/3].
  - **#11 failure training:** compounds 1–3 RIR, isolations to failure [T3, T6 vs T21]; Block 3 ramps toward failure late-cycle by design.
  - Rest standardized 2 min (vs 2–3 baseline [knowledge/02]) — 75-min budget at 19–23 sets/session requires it.
