# Golden Era

**Lifter:** app template (no profile — generic intermediate assumptions in §9) · **Level:** Intermediate (2–4 yrs) · **Goal:** hypertrophy · **Split:** Arnold split (Chest+Back / Shoulders+Arms / Legs ×2), 6 days/wk · **Session target:** 60 min · **Length:** 12 weeks = 2 blocks of (4 overload + 1 deload)

**What this program is:** The classic golden-era structure — chest paired with back, a dedicated shoulders-and-arms day, and a leg day, each hit twice a week with Sunday off. Antagonist pairing (pressing with pulling, biceps with triceps) keeps sessions short and recovery cheap while every muscle lands in the growth band at twice-weekly frequency. Progress runs the standard engine: add reps weekly for four weeks, deload, then add sets for four weeks, deload, and re-test. If a lift stalls two sessions in a row, drop one set from the exercise before it and rerun — the logbook decides, not the calendar.

## 1. Weekly schedule

| Day | Session |

| Mon | Chest+Back A |
| Tue | Shoulders+Arms A |
| Wed | Legs A |
| Thu | Chest+Back B |
| Fri | Shoulders+Arms B |
| Sat | Legs B |
| Sun | Rest |

Six consecutive training days exceed the "never more than 3 training days in a row" guideline [T20] — accepted deliberately as the price of the container, mitigated by design: the two lowest-systemic days (Shoulders+Arms) sit after the chest+back days, legs are split mid-week/late-week rather than stacked, and Sunday anchors a full rest before the heaviest press day [T5: hardest sessions after rest days]. Watch-item: if Wednesday squats feel heavy after Tuesday, swap Tue/Wed — the Arnold split tolerates day-order rotation.

## 4. Block structure & progression (12 weeks)

Intermediate: 4 overload + 1 deload per block [T6].

- **Block 1 — weeks 1–4 (Method A, reps):** week 1 calibrates at **RIR 3–4**; weeks 2–4 add **+1–2 reps per set** weekly at the same RIR; when a range tops out on all sets, double progression (add load next session, restart at range bottom) [T1, T2, T6].
- **Block 2 — weeks 5–9 (Method B, sets):** reps restart from Block-1 week-2 levels; add **+1 set/week** to one exercise per muscle (cap 5/exercise, then add an angle [T6]).
- **Weeks 5 and 10: deloads.** Week 12 end: re-test — week-1 loads on incline press, pull-up, squat, DB press; compare every rep.

## 5. Deload plan

Volume deload (weeks 5, 10): **halve sets, keep loads** — volume is this program's stressor [T15]. Sessions should feel easy; if the deload week still feels hard, you timed it right. Symptom override (soreness lingering, light weights heavy, no pump) pulls a deload forward regardless of week [T15, T20].

## 6. Autoregulation & adjustment triggers

- A lift's reps flat/down 2 straight sessions → prior volume too high: drop 1 set from the exercise before it, rerun [T18].
- Elbow niggles → swap angles first (preacher → Bayesian curl; skull-crusher-style → overhead cable [arms/2, arms/3]), cut a set second; never train through joint pain.
- 6-day weeks catching up with you (poor sleep, motivation dropping) → the frequency-deload option: spread the week over 8–10 days [T15].
- Progressing on all lifts? Change nothing [T16]. Log every set [T6].

## 7. Design rationale & contradictions

- **Decision-tree branch:** no condition fired — healthy intermediate, 6-day availability, split-type preference = Arnold-style body-part pairing. Honored per the consistency-beats-optimality meta-rule [T19–T22]; volume-per-muscle targets were set first and the container held them (meta-rule 1 [T7, T17]).
- **Volume:** every large muscle at 12 in the 10–20 band, each across exactly 2 sessions (6/session = the per-session quality plateau [T4, T22]); back far under the 25-set caution line [back/1].
- **Antagonist pairing** (press/pull supersets conceptually, bi/tri literally) preserves performance and saves time [T4, T23].
- **Contradictions touched** (knowledge/08 § Known Contradictions):
  - **#4 volume norms:** arms at 8 direct sets — between arms/1's 3–6 and T6's 20–30 small-muscle claim; position: mild above-band trial, justified by the container, kill-switch is the standard logbook rule.
  - **#9 rear delts:** both positions honored — rear delts open each shoulder session [shoulders/1, shoulders/6] but only 6 total sets, on the "back training covers them" logic [shoulders/2].
  - **#11 failure training:** compounds 1–3 RIR, isolations/machines last set to failure [T3, T6 vs T21]; Block-2 set-adding approaches T21-style effort late in the block.
  - **6 consecutive days vs [T20] ≤3-consecutive rule:** flagged in §1 with mitigation, not silently resolved.
  - Rest standardized 2 min (vs 2–3 baseline [knowledge/02]) — the 60-min budget at 6 days/wk requires it.
