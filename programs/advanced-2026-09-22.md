# Peak Growth

**Lifter:** [advanced](../profiles/advanced.md) · **Level:** Advanced (10+ yrs) · **Goal:** hypertrophy · **Split:** PPL + Upper/Lower hybrid, 5 days/wk · **Session target:** 75 min · **Length:** 12 weeks = 3 blocks of (3 overload + 1 deload)

**What this program is:** Five sessions a week — Push, Pull, Legs early on, then a Lower and Upper day on Friday/Saturday — so every muscle gets hit twice with different exercises each time. At this training age progress comes in small doses, so each block is only three overload weeks long before a deload, and the three blocks use three different progression levers: first reps, then sets, then pushing closer to failure. Expect to fight for one rep at a time; if a lift stalls two sessions straight, that's the signal to strip a set and rebuild, not to grind harder. Week 12 is a deload plus a re-test — the logged reps there tell you what the next program should be.

## 4. Block structure & progression (12 weeks)

Level matrix: advanced → ~3 overload weeks + 1 deload per block [T6]; +0–1 reps/wk; blocks alternate their lever.

- **Block 1 — weeks 1–4 (Method A, logbook-focused):** week 1 calibrates at **RIR 3**; weeks 2–3 add **+1 rep per set** weekly (small advanced jumps [T6]); double progression when a range tops out [T1, T2]. Week 4: deload.
- **Block 2 — weeks 5–8 (Method B, high-volume):** the between-block change is sets, not reps [T2, T6] — add 1 set/week to one upper and one lower exercise (cap 5/exercise, then a different exercise [T6]); reps restart from week-2 levels. Week 8: deload.
- **Block 3 — weeks 9–12 (Method C, effort ramp):** sets frozen at Block-2 levels; RPE ramps **7 → 8 → 9** across weeks 9–11 — reps rise as proximity to failure increases [T6]. Week 12: deload + **re-test** — repeat Block-1 week-2 loads on main lifts and compare logged reps; that comparison picks the next program [T2, T6].

## 5. Deload plan

Volume deload, matched to the stressor: **halve sets, keep loads** [T15]. Weeks 4, 8, and 12 — the advanced 3+1 rhythm [T6]. No new stressors during a deload; it should feel easy, and "if your light deload week still feels hard, you timed it right" [T15]. Symptom override: warning signs [T15, T20] pull the deload forward regardless of week.

## 6. Autoregulation & adjustment triggers

- An exercise's reps flat/down 2 straight sessions → prior volume too high: drop 1 set, rerun [T18, T8].
- Progressing → change nothing [T16].
- PR-less for 8 weeks on a main lift → exercise swap signal (after 2–5 blocks, or reps <6) [T6, T9–T11 context].
- Joint pain → swap to the listed alternative immediately [T6].
- Log every set of every session [T6]; the logbook judges — not soreness or pump [T16, T18, T19].

## 7. Design rationale & contradictions

- **Decision-tree branch:** advanced (10+ yrs), healthy, 5 fixed days, no priority → 5–6-day container: **U/L + PPL hybrid** (07-tree / container mapping). Note: full "inside-out" emergence [T7] not needed — no plateau complaints stated; template still fits (95% rule).
- **12-week validation** (level matrix): 12 = 3 × (3 overload + 1 deload) — exactly the advanced rhythm; a longer overload stretch would have been rejected.
- **Volume:** majors 10–15/wk, 2×/wk frequency each, per-session peaks ≤8 after redistribution [T4, T22]; back 15 under the 25 caution line [back/1].
- **Contradictions touched** (knowledge/08 § Known Contradictions):
  - **#9 rear delts:** "covered by back training" position [shoulders/2] over "prioritize first" [shoulders/3/4/6] — 15 back sets, no delt priority.
  - **#10 calves:** both positions preserved; minimal sets + stretch pauses [legs/1 vs legs/3].
  - **#11 failure training:** compounds 1–3 RIR, isolations to failure [T3, T6 vs T21]; Block 3's RPE ramp deliberately approaches the T21 position late in the cycle — stated, not averaged.
  - **#4 volume norms:** mid-band over the >10–12 ROI reading [T1/T6 vs T22] — growth goal, good recovery.
  - **#2/#3 split-family debates:** hybrid PPL+U/L chosen per the 5-day mapping [07-tree]; bro-split defense (T20) not applicable at 5 days.
  - Rest standardized 2 min (vs 2–3 baseline / 4–5 heavy legs [knowledge/02]) — session budget and consistency.
