# Boulder U/L

**Lifter:** app template (no profile — generic intermediate assumptions in §9) · **Level:** Intermediate (2–4 yrs) · **Goal:** hypertrophy, **shoulders + arms emphasis** · **Split:** Upper/Lower 4 days + dedicated Arms+Delts day, 5 days/wk · **Session target:** 60 min · **Length:** 12 weeks = 2 blocks of (4 overload + 1 deload)

**What this program is:** The intermediate default — a 4-day Upper/Lower — with a fifth Saturday bolted on that belongs entirely to delts and arms. The deal the split makes: the upper days drop their delt and arm work entirely (so chest and back stay tight and quick) and that volume cashes in on Saturday as five direct sets each of side delts, biceps, and triceps plus rear-delt and press work. Legs run untouched at 12 sets each so nothing else funds the emphasis. Progress is the standard engine: reps up for four weeks, deload, sets up for four weeks, deload, re-test. If Saturday arm numbers slide, cut a set there before touching anything else.

## 4. Block structure & progression (12 weeks)

Intermediate: 4 overload + 1 deload per block [T6].

- **Block 1 — weeks 1–4 (Method A, reps):** week 1 calibrates at **RIR 3–4**; weeks 2–4 add **+1–2 reps per set** weekly; double progression when a range tops out [T1, T2, T6].
- **Block 2 — weeks 5–9 (Method B, sets, delt-weighted):** add **+1 set/week to Saturday's delt and arm exercises first** (cap 5/exercise [T6]), then to any still-progressing upper lift. Progression targets side delts toward 12–16 priority volume by week 8 if recovery allows [shoulders/5].
- **Weeks 5 and 10: deloads.** Week 12 end: re-test week-1 loads on incline press, pull-up, squat, preacher curl, DB lateral.

## 5. Deload plan

Volume deload (weeks 5, 10): **halve sets, keep loads** [T15]. The Saturday session halves too — delt/arm emphasis is banked in recovery, not squeezed through it. Symptom override pulls a deload forward [T15, T20].

## 6. Autoregulation & adjustment triggers

- Monday incline-press reps sliding while everything else holds → Saturday triceps volume is crowding pressing recovery: drop Saturday pushdowns by 1 set (watch-item for lifters strong enough that pressing wants 4–5 sleeps [back/7]).
- A lift flat/down 2 straight sessions → cut 1 set from the exercise before it, rerun [T18].
- Elbow niggles → swap angles first (preacher → Bayesian; rope → cable cuff overhead) [arms/2]; never push through tendon pain [arms/3].
- Progressing on all lifts? Change nothing [T16]. Log every set [T6].

## 7. Design rationale & contradictions

- **Decision-tree branch:** healthy intermediate default → Upper/Lower, "the sweet spot for intermediates" [T22], scaled to 5 days; the fifth day is the container reshaping around a shoulders+arms emphasis (the 07 specialization logic applied at mild dose — emphasis, not specialization: no strong area dropped below growth volume).
- **Volume:** delts/arms concentrated on a fresh dedicated day rather than sprinkled across fatigued upper-day tails — the distribution-quality argument [T18] applied to the emphasis muscles; large muscles untouched at 10–12 so nothing funds the emphasis by atrophy.
- **Contradictions touched** (knowledge/08 § Known Contradictions):
  - **#9 rear delts:** both positions stated — 3 first-in-session sets on the [shoulders/1] scheduling side, 3-total on the [shoulders/2] coverage side; dial documented in the audit bullets.
  - **#4 volume norms:** arms kept inside 3–6 [arms/1] rather than trialing 20–30 [T6] — this is the conservative position because the emphasis here is delts-first.
  - **#11 failure training:** compounds 1–3 RIR; Saturday's machines/isolations take last sets to failure [T3, T6 vs T21].
  - Rest standardized 2 min (vs 2–3 baseline [knowledge/02]) — the 60-min budget requires it.

## 8. Assumptions

App template — no intake interview run. Defaults applied:

- Generic intermediate lifter (2–4 yrs structured training, progressing, no complaints) → intermediate row of the level matrix throughout
- Full commercial gym, no injuries, maintenance calories, good recovery, fixed 5-day schedule with Sat/Sun off
- 60-min session cap
- No current-program Step 0 audit (template) → volumes start mid-band; the logbook calibrates [07 § Step 0]
- Side delts treated as the first-among-equals emphasis, arms second — a lifter who wants arms-first should run the arms specialization template instead [arms/3]
