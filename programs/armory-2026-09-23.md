# Armory

**Lifter:** [armory](../profiles/armory.md) · **Level:** Advanced (10+ yrs) · **Goal:** hypertrophy, **arms specialization** · **Split:** Torso/Limb, 6 days/wk (arms 4×/wk, no dedicated arm day) · **Session target:** 60 min · **Length:** 12 weeks = 3 blocks of (3 overload + 1 deload)

**What this program is:** Six short sessions a week, alternating torso days (chest, back, delts) with limb days where arms come *first*, while they're fresh — that's the whole specialization trick: you don't add an arm day, you give arms four first-class hits a week (13 direct sets each) while chest and back keep their normal 16. Everything runs in three-week waves: push reps for three weeks, deload, add sets for three weeks, deload, then train closer to failure for three weeks, deload and re-test. If arm measurements and arm-lift reps aren't moving by week 8, the volume trial failed and arms drop back to maintenance — that's the logbook deciding, not hope.

The T8 torso/limb template is a 6-day week [T8, 07-tree arms branch]. This exceeds the "never more than 3 training days in a row" bro-split guideline [T20] — accepted deliberately, mitigated by design: limb days are low-systemic (arms + single leg movements), limb days sit between every torso day, and heavy pressing is spaced Mon/Fri with a machine-led press day Wednesday to respect pressing-recovery needs once strong [back/7]. Watch-item: if Wed presses still feel heavy, drop Wednesday pressing to 2 sets — that's the built-in release valve.

## 4. Block structure & progression (12 weeks)

Level matrix: advanced → 3 overload + 1 deload per block [T6]; levers rotate between blocks.

- **Block 1 — weeks 1–4 (Method A, reps):** week 1 calibrates at **RIR 3**; weeks 2–3 add **+1 rep per set** weekly (advanced jumps are small [T6]); double progression when a range tops out [T1, T2]. Arms included — every rep on preacher curls and overhead extensions is logged.
- **Block 2 — weeks 5–8 (Method B, sets — arm-weighted):** add 1 set/week **to arm exercises first** (cap 5/exercise [T6]), then to any torso lift still progressing; reps restart from Block-1 week-2 levels. Week 8: deload.
- **Block 3 — weeks 9–12 (Method C, effort ramp):** sets frozen; RPE ramps **7 → 8 → 9** across weeks 9–11 [T6]. Week 12: deload + **re-test** — week-1 loads on preacher curl, overhead extension, incline press, pull-up; arm measurements alongside. The re-test plus the week-8 checkpoint (below) decide whether arms stay specialized.

## 5. Deload plan

Volume deload, matched to the stressor: **halve sets, keep loads** [T15]. Weeks 4, 8, 12 — the advanced 3+1 rhythm [T6]. In this program the deload also halves arm volume — recovery is where the specialization is actually banked. Symptom override pulls a deload forward regardless of week [T15, T20]; with six training days, take the early deload rather than pushing.

## 6. Autoregulation & adjustment triggers

- **Specialization kill-switch (unique to this program):** if arm-lift reps and measurements haven't moved by **week 8**, the high-volume trial failed [T18, contradiction #4] — cut arms to 6 sets/wk maintenance [T22], return the freed sets to torso work, and re-specialize something else next cycle.
- Elbow or shoulder niggles from arm volume → swap angles first ( preacher → Bayesian curl; pushdown → cross-body extension [arms/1, T5]), cut a set second, never train through joint pain [T6].
- An exercise's reps flat/down 2 straight sessions → prior volume too high: drop 1 set, rerun [T18, T8-knowledge].
- Progressing → change nothing [T16]. Log every set [T6]; the logbook judges [T16, T18, T19].

## 7. Design rationale & contradictions

- **Decision-tree branch:** "Arms are the priority?" → Torso/Limb split run 6 days/wk, arms 4×/wk, no dedicated arm day [T8 via 07-tree]. Q21 free-text preference (torso/limb) consistent.
- **12-week validation** (level matrix): 3 × (3+1) advanced rhythm; arms specialization is a legitimate advanced tool [T17, T18, T21].
- **Volume:** specialization by reallocation — arms 13 funded from a conservative torso allocation (16 chest/back), total systemic volume held near a standard 6-day program.
- **Contradictions touched** (knowledge/08 § Known Contradictions):
  - **#4 volume norms:** T6's small-muscle 20–30 claim vs T22's >10–12 ROI caution — resolved toward a *time-boxed trial at 13* with an explicit week-8 kill-switch; positions stated, not averaged.
  - **#9 rear delts:** 7 sets, "back training covers them" side [shoulders/2] with 16 back sets — insurance sets retained.
  - **#10 calves:** both positions preserved; minimal sets + stretch pauses [legs/1 vs legs/3].
  - **#11 failure training:** compounds 1–3 RIR, arm isolations to failure [T3, T6 vs T21]; Block 3 approaches the failure position late-cycle by design.
  - **6-day frequency vs [T20] ≤3-consecutive rule:** T8 template taken as given; mitigation (limb-day placement, machine-led Wednesday) stated in §1 — flagged, not silently resolved.
  - Rest standardized 2 min (vs 2–3 baseline [knowledge/02]) — the 60-min budget at 6 days/wk requires it.
