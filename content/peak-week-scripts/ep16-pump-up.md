# Ep 16 — The 10-Minute Pump-Up
**Arc IV, episode 2 of 3 · Target length 60–80 s · Talking head + backstage b-roll (fast-paced)**

## Hook (0–3s)
- **Spoken:** "The guy pumping up for 35 minutes backstage? He's getting FLATTER while he does it."
- **ON-SCREEN:** "35 MIN = FLATTER."
- **VISUAL:** Backstage b-roll: athlete cranking band pull-aparts, sweating; timer graphic running up.

## Script

| Time | VO | ON-SCREEN | VISUAL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Over-pumping is the number-one backstage mistake. Here's the mechanism, then the fix. | "Over-pumping = mistake #1" | Creator to camera, backstage wall of trophies behind |
| 0:05–0:18 | A pump burns glycogen. And when glycogen burns, water leaves with it — so a 35-minute pump session leaves you flatter and looking spilled, while the athlete who pumped correctly keeps filling ON stage. | "Glycogen burns → water leaves" | Animated muscle cell losing volume; split-screen fading vs filling physiques |
| 0:18–0:30 | The fix is timing: start your pump when the class BEFORE yours walks on stage. That's roughly 15–20 minutes out — too early flattens you, and rushing kills the pump. | "Start when the class BEFORE goes on" | POV: curtain, emcee announcing previous class; "GO" trigger graphic |
| 0:30–0:42 | What it looks like: push-ups, sipping Gatorade — a fast carb. In 10–15 minutes you're veinier, fuller, you hold poses better, and you keep filling while everyone else fades. | "10–15 min: push-ups + Gatorade" | Push-up b-roll in board shorts; Gatorade sip; forearm veins macro |
| 0:42–0:52 | Pump everything EXCEPT legs. Pumping legs stores blood and fluid down there — it hides your cuts and separations exactly where you need them most. | "Everything BUT legs" | Red X over leg press; green check over push-ups/bands/press-ups |
| 0:52–1:05 | Can't get a pump at all? It's almost 1,000% a sodium issue — more salt, and it comes back in about 20 minutes. And remember: in Meadows' words, you can't pump up a dehydrated muscle — a muscle is mostly water. | "No pump = salt fix, ~20 min" | Salt packet tear macro; water bottle chug; pump returning b-roll |
| 1:05–1:15 | Last thing: stay warm. AC venues cool you down fast — deep heat, even a hair dryer, because you can't put a jumper on over the top tan coat. | "Stay warm: deep heat, hair dryer" | Hair dryer on shoulders backstage; deep-heat rub b-roll |
| 1:15–1:20 | Nail that, and you walk out full. What happens AFTER the trophy — that's the finale. | "Next: the post-show rebound" | Walk-out-to-stage silhouette, stage lights flare |
