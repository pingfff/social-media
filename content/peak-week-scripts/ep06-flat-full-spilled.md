## Caption
Every peak-week adjustment starts with one diagnosis: flat, full, or spilled.
Spills show in the midsection first, usually mean a sodium/water issue — not a carb one, and vascularity while flat means you still have room.
Tune small: +20 g carbs / +6 g fat per meal; scale jumps → repeat the last meal.
General education, not medical advice.
