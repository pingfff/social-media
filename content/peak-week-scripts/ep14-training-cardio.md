# Ep 14 — Training & Cardio During Peak Week (Everyone Does Too Much)
**Arc III → Arc IV bridge · Target length 70–85 s · Talking head + gym b-roll**

## Hook (0–3s)
- **Spoken:** "The biggest mistake in peak week isn't food. It's the extra workout you 'earned.'"
- **ON-SCREEN:** "TOO MUCH TRAINING."
- **VISUAL:** Creator mid-frame in gym; b-roll of someone grinding a set to failure, replayed twice with a red X.

## Script

| Time | VO | ON-SCREEN | VISUAL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | You're 7 days out and motivated — that's exactly the problem. Athletes overtrain in peak week out of fear. | "Fear = overtraining" | Gym b-roll, clock/overlay counting down "7 DAYS" |
| 0:05–0:18 | The rule from the coaches: start the deload about 7 days out. Cut volume ~30%, drop a set per exercise, stay at RIR 2–3 — never to failure. The stimulus now only maintains tissue; the fatigue is not worth it. | "−30% volume · RIR 2–3 · NO failure" | Whiteboard graphic with the three rules; light set stopped 2–3 reps short, green check |
| 0:18–0:30 | Hard rule number two: NO soreness. Muscle damage blocks glycogen uptake — if you're sore Friday trying to fill your chest out, tough. You can't fill a muscle you just damaged. | "Sore = can't fill" | Split visual: damaged muscle fiber graphic vs glycogen flowing into an intact one |
| 0:30–0:45 | Structure it: legs EARLY in the week, so inflammation doesn't blur your leg definition by Saturday. Mid-week, light pump-style work — 2–3 sets per body part, ~6 reps, to shuttle glycogen. Friday: no training. Posing is the only workout left. | "Legs early · light pumps mid-week · Fri = posing only" | Timeline graphic Mon→Sat with leg icon early, posing mirror shot Friday |
| 0:45–0:58 | Cardio? Convert it to steps: 10 minutes of moderate cardio ≈ 2,000 steps. Target an easy 7–10k a day and taper cardio off by Wednesday. | "10 min cardio ≈ 2,000 steps" | Step counter climbing on phone screen; treadmill crossed out → walking b-roll |
| 0:58–1:10 | The trap nobody sees: at 2 days out you're walking around all day; at 1 day out you're sitting in a hotel. Your activity silently craters and your Friday changes stop making sense. Standardize a low, consistent step count all week. | "Hotel sitting = silent drop" | Split: busy expo hallway vs lonely hotel room; step graph falling off a cliff |
| 1:10–1:20 | Drop the fatigue, hold the look. Tomorrow: show day — where sodium, not carbs, is the lever. | "Next: show-day sodium" | Creator to camera; cut to alarm clock hitting show-day morning |
