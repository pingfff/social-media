# Instagram Series: Bodybuilding Peak Week — Complete Series Plan

*Built from `knowledge/bodybuilding/01–06`. Citations: [PW#] transcripts, [W#] websites in `Program Design/Peak Week/`.*

**Series logic:** four arcs — (I) the fundamentals of the three levers,
(II) the looks on stage and the loading strategies, (III) the failure modes,
(IV) show day and aftermath. Each episode: one idea → mechanism → actionable rule,
ending with a hook to the next episode.

## Arc I — The Three Levers

### Ep 1 — What peak week actually is (series anchor)

- Hook: "Peak week can improve a great physique by 5%. It can ruin one by 50%."
- You can't do much to look drastically better, but you can easily look worse [PW13]
- Only three variables matter: carbs, water, sodium/potassium [W1]
- Goal = max muscle glycogen + water in the muscle + balanced electrolytes;
  the enemy is subcutaneous water and spillover [W1]
- "Nobody ever says 'I looked like crap a week out but peak week fixed everything'" [PW12]
- The non-negotiable: shredded BEFORE peak week or nothing matters [PW1][PW12]

### Ep 2 — Carbs 101: why carbs make you "full"

- Every gram of glycogen binds ~3 g water (cited range 2–4 g) [PW4][PW7][PW13][W1]
- Fullness = carbs + water + sodium; muscle pushes against the skin like a
  water balloon [PW7][W1]
- Total capacity ≈ 23 g/kg lean tissue (~2,300 g for a 100 kg male); 180-lb human
  stores ~350–500 g muscle + 60–120 g liver glycogen [PW8][W1]
- Assimilation takes 24–48 h — show-day carbs can't finish the job [W2][PW18]
- After a long prep you're extremely insulin sensitive — carbs preferentially
  fill glycogen, not fat [W1]

### Ep 3 — Sodium 101: the most misunderstood mineral

- Sodium is the transport system for carbs (intestinal glucose transporter is
  sodium-dependent) — no salt, no fill, plus gut bloat [PW11][PW12]
- Sodium raises blood pressure acutely → better pumps [PW13][W1]
- Cut it and the Army study happens: blood sodium unchanged, aldosterone doubles
  in 2 days, triples in 3 — rebound lands on show day [PW12]
- The rule: keep it identical meal-to-meal all week, scale it UP with the carb
  load [PW2][PW3][PW7][PW18]

### Ep 4 — Potassium: the one you should NOT load

- Loading potassium while cutting sodium is "stupid and useless" [PW12]
- The danger: excess K hyperpolarizes cardiac cells — lethal injection is
  potassium chloride [PW12][PW15]
- Keep normal fruit/veg sources; Morton Lite Salt (50/50 Na/K) is the safe tool [PW15][PW18]

### Ep 5 — Water 101: "hydrated equals striated"

- Muscle is ~70–75% water; "a flat muscle is a soft muscle, a full muscle is a
  hard muscle" [PW2][PW13][PW18]
- Drinking more doesn't make you hold water — output scales with intake; excess
  is urinated out [PW7][PW15][W1]
- You cannot over-hydrate without excess carbs [PW15]
- Steady 1.5–2 gal/day; at no point should you feel dehydrated in peak week [W1]
- Consistency beats manipulation: same amount daily so Friday changes are
  predictable [PW2]

## Arc II — The Looks & The Strategies

### Ep 6 — Flat, full, or spilled: reading your own physique

- Flat = dehydrated/small/soft/light; full = muscle against the skin, hard;
  spilled = subcutaneous water, soft, washed out [PW3]
- Spills show first in the midsection [PW3]
- Spillover = more carbs than storage capacity; glucose spills interstitial,
  water follows [W1][PW9]
- Spills are usually a sodium/water problem, not carbs — don't train to burn
  one off [PW8]
- Vascularity = gauge of approaching the spill line (vascular while flat = safe) [PW14]
- The J3U tuning method: add +20 g carbs/+6 g fat per meal; if scale jumps,
  repeat the previous meal [PW3]

### Ep 7 — Water & sodium manipulation: what the traditional protocol does

- The classic: load 2+ gal early week → keto depletion → Thursday sodium cut →
  Friday dry-carb load → last-day water cut [PW4][PW1]
- Why it "works" on paper: 1 g carb : 3 g water; 3–6 lb swings in 3 days [PW4]
- Ramp rule if you try it: never jump straight to 2 gal — add a bottle a day
  the week before [PW4]
- Why the modern consensus rejects it: compartments move 1:1, aldosterone
  rebounds on stage day, dehydration kills vascularity [PW7][PW12]
- Feather's moderate version: raise water ~1.25× early, return to normal Thu–Fri
  so you diurese while carbs pull fluid in — the "full/dry" Friday-night window [PW8]

### Ep 8 — Front loading

- High carbs Monday (deliberate overshoot to find the spill line), taper down,
  small bump 1–2 days out [PW11][W2]
- Advantage: a 3–4 day correction window [W2]
- Flaw: best look arrives Tue/Wed and fades; the Monday refeed ramps suppressed
  metabolism → weight drops all week [PW11]
- Best for: bikini, figure, wellness [W2]

### Ep 9 — Back loading (+ rapid back load)

- 3–4 days very low carb, rapid load final 2–3 days; supercompensation up to
  show day; no water/sodium cutting needed [W2]
- Cliff Wilson's rapid back load: 4–5 day depletion at near-normal calories →
  >800 g carbs in the final 24 h with timed potassium/sodium; Rico's real
  numbers: 1,000 g across 12 meals every 1.5 h [W2][PW1]
- High risk, high reward, near-zero correction time; requires extreme leanness
  and an experienced coach with daily check-ins [PW11][W2]

### Ep 10 — Linear progressive loading (the modern default)

- Klemczewski's balloon analogy; start moderate, add every day — "usually the
  highest carb day of prep is the day before the show" [PW11][PW9]
- Revelia's numbers: ~275 g Monday, +40–50 g/day, adjusted on the daily look [PW11]
- Why it beats the Friday refeed: metabolism "kicks on" from gradual refeeds;
  athletes drop weight while calories rise [PW9]
- Prerequisite: already shredded + carb-response data from prep [PW9][W2]
- Verdict: minimal risk, high reward, excellent predictability [W2]

## Arc III — Failure Modes

### Ep 11 — Why you're "holding water" (it's not the water)

- Fatigue/cortisol model: diet + training + cardio + bad sleep + stress →
  cortisol → same receptor as aldosterone → retention [PW6]
- "Dropping fatigue is going to mean dropping water" [PW6]
- Peak-week "whooshes" aren't new fat loss — cortisol water leaving [PW6]
- "I was holding water on stage" = you weren't lean enough [PW12]

### Ep 12 — The guru protocol autopsy

- Deplete + load water/sodium early, cut both late, load potassium, diuretic,
  stop training [PW12]
- Result: flat, can't pump, best look a week early [PW12]
- Post-show cheat meal "magic" = sodium + water freedom, not carbs [PW12]
- Fish-and-asparagus skin thinning has no physiology — thinner skin = leaner [W1]

### Ep 13 — Diuretics: the horror stories

- Full tab morning of = "done 99% of the time" [PW5]
- Renal failure from Dyazide with no fluids; backstage collapse, medics twice [PW5]
- "For a plastic trophy — you could die" [PW12]
- Meadows' coach flushed his down the toilet → top-5 finish, best early career
  look [PW2]

### Ep 14 — Training & cardio during peak week (everyone does too much)

- Deload ~7 days out, ~30% volume cut, RIR 2–3, never failure [PW6]
- NO soreness — muscle damage blocks glycogen uptake [PW8]
- Legs early in the week; light pump work late; posing is the only "training"
  Friday [PW1][PW8][PW13]
- Cardio → steps (10 min ≈ 2,000 steps); beware the silent activity drop when
  you sit in a hotel 1 day out [PW6][PW3]

## Arc IV — Show Day & Aftermath

### Ep 15 — Show day: sodium is the lever, not carbs

- Glycogen takes 24–48 h; nothing backstage finishes today [W2][PW18]
- Wake flat = sodium + water: first meal + 16–20 oz water brings vascularity [PW9]
- Sodium window 30–60 min pre-stage [PW18]; "no pump = 1,000% a sodium issue" [PW19]
- Over-carbing "because I'm flat" = spillover, "morbidly soft" an hour after
  looking super tight [PW16][PW19]
- Night show: big sodium meal + optional 30-min workout between sessions [PW9][W1]

### Ep 16 — The 10-minute pump-up

- 35-min pumps burn glycogen; water leaves with it — you fade on stage [PW8][PW16]
- Start when the class before yours goes on; push-ups + Gatorade [PW8][PW13]
- Pump everything EXCEPT legs [PW13]
- Stay warm: deep heat, hair dryer, cold AC venues [PW13]

### Ep 17 — Post-show: don't undo the rebound

- Cheat-meal fixation mimics eating-disorder behavior; hunger-dominated preps
  were wrong [PW8]
- That night: water + veg → clean carbs a few times a day → the muffin →
  restaurant meal a week later [PW8]
- Back to maintenance, not binge or crawl-up reverse dieting [PW8]
- "The diet lasts one week longer than the show."
