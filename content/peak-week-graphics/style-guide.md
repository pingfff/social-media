# Peak Week Series — Graphics Style Guide

Shared visual system for all on-screen graphics in `content/peak-week-scripts/`.
Output: PNG, 1080×1080 (feed) — can be overlaid on 1080×1920 reels with
letterboxing or exported at story size later.

## Canvas

- Background: near-black `#111318`, subtle vignette.
- Content margin: 90 px safe zone all sides.
- Footer strip: episode tag (e.g. "EP 2 · CARBS 101") small caps, muted grey,
  bottom-left; optional handle bottom-right.

## Type

- Headline: heavy condensed sans (Helvetica/Arial Bold Condensed fallback),
  all caps, white, ~90–120 px.
- Emphasis words: accent color, same weight.
- Body/labels: regular sans, `#C9CED6`, 34–44 px.
- Numbers/stats: oversized (150–200 px) accent color.

## Color coding (used consistently across ALL episodes)

- Carbs: amber `#F5A623`
- Water: blue `#3FA7F5`
- Sodium/salt: coral `#F55B5B`
- Potassium: violet `#9B6BF5`
- Positive/"full" state: green `#3FD68C`
- Negative/flat/spill state: grey `#7A828E` (flat) / red-tint `#E04848` (danger)
- Timeline/show-day marker: white with amber underline

## Graphic archetypes (map script suggestions to these)

1. **Equation card** — "FULL = CARBS + WATER + SODIUM" style, colored tokens.
2. **Timeline strip** — Mon→Sat calendar with per-day carb/water bars; red
   "SHOW DAY" marker.
3. **Line chart** — e.g. aldosterone 2×/3× spike with show-day marker.
4. **Comparison split** — FLAT | FULL | SPILLED side-by-side panels.
5. **Stat card** — one giant number ("~3 g water per 1 g glycogen").
6. **Warning card** — red border, sober tone (Ep 4/13 only).
